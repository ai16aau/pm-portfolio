03 · About

Strategy, product and data

I'm Ayodele Imhotep. I sit across strategy, product and data rather than in any one of them, and I'm happiest where the three meet: working out what a product should do for the people using it, then proving it with numbers.

My background spans management consulting and strategy and operations in global payments, where I own metrics and reporting across a large control framework and build the dashboards and semantic models that senior stakeholders run on. Alongside that, I design and ship my own products end to end, from the first persona to the instrumentation plan, and write the honest version of how each release went. That written-up honesty is what this portfolio is for.
